OUR STORY

Why did we start Skill Up?

01

The idea started with a simple problem.

As students ourselves, we have experienced the gap between what we learn in college and what the industry expects.

Students often struggle to identify the right skills, choose a suitable career path and find the right learning resources.

That's why we created Skill Up — a platform designed to make the journey from campus to career simpler and clearer.

THE CHALLENGE

The Problem We Saw

Students have opportunities, but finding the right direction can be difficult.

01

Too Many Choices

Students often have multiple career options but don't know which path matches their interests and skills.

02

Skill Confusion

It can be difficult to understand which skills are actually required for a particular career or job role.

03

Industry Gap

What students learn academically and what companies expect can sometimes be very different.
